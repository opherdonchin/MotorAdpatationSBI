## Session 2 — Confirming the one-state model (decision 0002)

**Goal:** settle the open points of decision 0002 so the one-state analysis can be scoped.

**Done:**
- The session-start hook delivered the orientation data in a real new session.
- Opher answered the three open points; decision
  [0002](../decisions/0002-one-state-model.md) and the plan were updated on `main`.

**Results:** reading "the mass" as a central 95% interval gives
$\operatorname{logit} A \sim \mathcal N(4.00, 1.48)$ (median 0.982),
$\operatorname{logit} B \sim \mathcal N(-2.30, 1.17)$ (median 0.091), and a log-normal
block length with median 22 trials. One-off calculation with `scipy`, not kept.

**Decisions:**
- Initial state $x_1 \sim \mathcal N(0, \sigma_x^2)$ instead of a known $x_0 = 0$; the
  Kalman filter starts from $m_1 = 0$, $P_1 = \sigma_x^2$.
- Domains $A, B \in (0,1)$, $\sigma_x, \sigma_y > 0$; priors on $A$ and $B$ normal on the
  log odds with the mass in $(0.75, 0.999)$ and $(0.01, 0.5)$.
- No fixed schedule: sittings of varying length, blocks of 0, +1 or -1 with lengths
  mostly between 10 and 50 trials and random transitions.

**Open questions / next:** four points of 0002 remain (the 95% reading, block
boundaries, sitting length, priors on the noise SDs); the scope issue for the one-state
analysis waits for them.
