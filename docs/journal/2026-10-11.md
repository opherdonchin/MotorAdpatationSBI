### Later still: the two-state Model, decision and Simulator

**Done:**
- [Issue #28](https://github.com/opherdonchin/MotorAdpatationSBI/issues/28) agreed by Opher in chat, with answers to its four points;
  the issue text now holds them.
- [PR #29](https://github.com/opherdonchin/MotorAdpatationSBI/pull/29): decision record 0008, the two-state Model and its Prior. The
  form was read from the public model code of Hulst et al. (2020),
  [thomashulst/paper-slowlearning](https://github.com/thomashulst/paper-slowlearning);
  the lab's private repository was not opened.
- [PR #30](https://github.com/opherdonchin/MotorAdpatationSBI/pull/30), built on #29: `src/models/two_state.py` (Simulator and Prior
  Sampler), 24 code tests, `docs/models/two_state.md`, and
  `notebooks/working/two_state_simulator.ipynb`. The three one-state working notebooks
  are removed on that branch, as Opher asked.

**Results** (`two_state_simulator.ipynb`, run at `b15ceca`):
- Equal retentions, no noise: two-state and one-state Simulators differ by 1.4e-11.
- Noise-free states against the closed form: 1.1e-11.
- Spontaneous recovery: movement +0.14 on the first no-vision Trial, -0.29 nine Trials
  later.
- Steady-state standard deviation and lag-1 correlation against a Lyapunov solution:
  largest of 16 z-scores 2.0.
- Prior quantiles match decision 0008; 12 unstable draws in a million before the redraw.

**Decisions:**
- Opher: one planning noise shared by the two states; the Model matches the lab's form
  (Hulst et al.); the states are ordered through positive differences on the logit
  scale (`A1_slow = A1_fast + diff`, `B1_slow = B1_fast - diff`), as in the lab's PyMC
  fits; the second notebook copies the first one's code, helpers discussed afterwards;
  older working notebooks go when the new branch is created; the finalized two-state
  notebook need not be documented as fully as the one-state one.
- Mine, awaiting Opher in the reviews: the numbers of the four rate priors; unstable
  Prior draws are drawn again; each state starts from a draw with the planning noise's
  standard deviation; parameter names `A_fast`, `A_slow`, `B_fast`, `B_slow`, with the
  naming exception in `AGENTS.md` widened to allow them.

**Surprises:**
- The one-state Model is not a special case of this one: with a shared planning noise
  neither state can be switched off. Check 1 of the issue was reworded to the link that
  holds (equal retentions).
- The proposed Prior lets the two time constants overlap (slow down to 6 Trials, fast up
  to 20).

**Open questions:**
- Whether the Prior's numbers, and that overlap, are what Opher wants.
- The posterior network will need the transforms between the six parameters and the six
  unbounded quantities. Where they live (a function in the Model module would be a new
  helper) is to be agreed before the third pull request.
