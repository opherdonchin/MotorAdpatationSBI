**Findings:**
- BayesFlow handles Sittings of different lengths natively: pad to a common length and
  pass a mask under the adapter key `summary_mask`; `TimeSeriesTransformer` and
  `FusionTransformer` use it (their `call` takes `mask`), and both the posterior and the
  ratio approximators pass it on. `TimeSeriesNetwork` (convolution plus recurrent) takes
  no mask.
- One-off feasibility probe on the saved demo Experiment (2,000 Sittings, padded length
  2,266; script not kept), base-size networks, flow matching, batch 32, 8 GB GPU:
  `FusionTransformer` without downsampling ran out of memory (11.5 GB requested).
  With downsampling it runs: `TimeSeriesTransformer(downsample=4)` 34 ms per step,
  `downsample=8` 11 ms per step, `FusionTransformer(downsample=8)` 29 ms per step. At
  34 ms per step, 20,000 Sittings for 100 epochs is about 35 minutes.
- Open risk: downsampling pools neighbouring Trials, and the information about the two
  noises is partly in the correlation between successive Trials. Whether it survives
  has to be tested, not assumed.
