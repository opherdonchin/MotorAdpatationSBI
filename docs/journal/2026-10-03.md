  1,475 trials (central 95%), median 833.

**Findings:**
- Equation 10 of van der Vliet et al. (2018) as printed (R(1) with vision) does not match
  simulation: it gives 0.14 to 0.39 where the simulation gives -0.13 to -0.04 for three of
  four parameter sets. A derived version, (A - B) V_x - B sigma_eps^2 over sigma_y^2,

  Whether the paper's figures used a correct version is not checked.
- With vision, successive movements are negatively correlated when B is large relative
  to the noise ratio; without vision always positively. That contrast is what no-vision
  blocks add.
