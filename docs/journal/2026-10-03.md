**Results** (one-off calculation with NumPy and SciPy, not kept):
- A symmetric Dirichlet over four kinds has Beta(a, 3a) marginals. No concentration
  makes each single proportion range over 0.1 to 0.7: at a = 2, P(< 0.1) = 0.15 and
  P(> 0.7) = 0.004; at a = 3, 0.09 and 0.0006. At a = 2 the largest proportion in a
  sitting is below 0.67 in 97.5% of sittings, and the median smallest is 0.10.
- 8 to 60 blocks with the existing log-normal block lengths give sittings of 208 to
  1,475 trials (central 95%), median 833.

**Decisions:**
