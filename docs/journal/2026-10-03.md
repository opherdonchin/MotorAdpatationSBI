**Results** (one-off calculation with NumPy and SciPy, not kept):

  sitting is below 0.67 in 97.5% of sittings, and the median smallest is 0.10.
- 8 to 60 blocks with the existing log-normal block lengths give sittings of 208 to
  1,475 trials (central 95%), median 833.

**Decisions:**
- No vision is a block kind, not a per-trial event (Opher).
- Sittings of about 200 to 1,500 trials (Opher); 8 to 60 blocks (Claude, to match).
- Dirichlet concentration 2 (Claude; Opher asked to confirm or give the value he had).

**Findings:**
- Equation 10 of van der Vliet et al. (2018) as printed (R(1) with vision) does not match
  simulation: it gives 0.14 to 0.39 where the simulation gives -0.13 to -0.04 for three of
  four parameter sets. A derived version, (A - B) V_x - B sigma_eps^2 over sigma_y^2,
  matches. The printed one has +B sigma_eps^2 and A^k (A - B)^k in the denominator.
  Whether the paper's figures used a correct version is not checked.
- With vision, successive movements are negatively correlated when B is large relative
  to the noise ratio; without vision always positively. That contrast is what no-vision
  blocks add.
